# QRTX 2023 Annual Filing (Synthetic)

## Business
QRTX operates a digital payments platform with two segments: Merchant Processing and Consumer Wallet. Key markets include Europe and Latin America. Revenue grew due to higher transaction volumes and new merchant onboarding.

## Risk Factors
- Payment fraud and cybersecurity incidents could result in financial losses, data breaches, and loss of merchant trust.
- Failure to maintain payment licenses and comply with anti-money laundering rules could trigger regulatory penalties.
- A significant portion of our revenue comes from a small number of large merchants.
- Interchange fee caps and pricing pressure from competing processors could compress take rates.
- Rising interest rates may increase funding costs for settlement float.
- Foreign exchange volatility across euro and Latin American currencies could reduce reported revenue.

## MD&A
Management discusses transaction volume growth, take-rate trends, and operating leverage. Notable trends include stabilizing take rates and investments in fraud-detection models.

## Legal Proceedings
A regulator in one European market opened an inquiry into our anti-money laundering controls, and we are cooperating fully. We cannot predict the outcome. Other proceedings are routine.
